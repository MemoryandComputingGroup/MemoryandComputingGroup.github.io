Postdoctoral researchers

Postdoctoral Positions / 博士后招聘

The Memory and Computing Group invites outstanding postdoctoral researchers to join us in advancing emerging memory devices, intelligent algorithms, and neuromorphic chips.

English

English position listing

Position Information

Research Areas

Nonvolatile memory devices and integration; neural-network algorithms and hardware co-design; computing-in-memory and neuromorphic chip design.

Openings

Two to three positions are available, with one position in each of the device, algorithm, and circuit-design areas.

Position Description

Postdoctoral researchers will conduct full-time research on emerging memory devices, algorithms, and chip design. Research may include optimizing and densely integrating emerging nonvolatile memory devices; developing neural-network algorithms and deploying them on hardware; and designing intelligent chips based on emerging memory technologies.

Qualifications

  1. Applicants should hold, or expect to receive, a Ph.D. in microelectronics, integrated circuits, computer science, semiconductors, or a related field. Applicants should demonstrate strong academic and personal performance, effective communication and coordination skills, good health, and normally be under 35 years of age.
  2. Applicants should have strong English communication and academic-writing skills, a commitment to research, effective interpersonal skills, and a collaborative spirit. Applicants should have published at least two high-quality journal papers as first author during their doctoral studies or within the past two years.

Salary and Benefits

  1. The group provides sufficient research funding, a stable research environment, and access to first-class research facilities. Outstanding postdoctoral researchers may receive short-term international exchange opportunities and support in applying for relevant postdoctoral programs.
  2. The group offers competitive compensation and supports applications to national, Shanghai, and university-level “Super Postdoctoral” programs. The pre-tax annual salary package is RMB 370,000–600,000 or above.
  3. Postdoctoral accommodation, household registration, and children’s school enrollment are administered in accordance with the University’s relevant policies.
  4. Outstanding postdoctoral researchers may receive priority recommendation for publicly advertised positions at the University.

How to Apply

Please email a single PDF containing your curriculum vitae, major research achievements, and research interests to xumengzhang@fudan.edu.cn. Please use the following subject line:

Postdoctoral Application + University + Full Name

招聘信息

研究方向

非易失存储器件与集成、神经网络算法与硬件协同、存算/类脑芯片设计。

招聘数量

2–3名，器件、算法、电路方向各1名。

职位描述

全职从事新型存储器件、算法以及芯片设计方面的研究，主要包括新型非易失存储器件优化及高密度集成、神经网络算法开发及硬件部署、基于新型存储器的智能芯片设计等。

应聘条件

  1. 具有或即将获得微电子、集成电路、计算机、半导体等相关领域的博士学位，品学兼优，有较好的沟通协调能力，身体健康，年龄在35周岁以下。
  2. 拥有良好的英语表达能力和论文写作能力，热爱工作，善于沟通，具有团队精神。博士期间或近两年以第一作者发表高水平期刊论文2篇及以上。

薪酬与福利待遇

  1. 课题组为博士后提供充足的科研经费、稳定的工作环境、一流的科研平台，为优秀者提供短期国际交流机会，协助申报博士后相关项目。
  2. 课题组为博士后提供具有竞争力的薪资，协助申请国家/上海/学校“超博计划”（税前年薪37–60万+）。
  3. 博士后公寓、户口和子女入学按照学校相关政策执行。
  4. 博后期间表现优秀者，可优先推荐应聘学校公开招聘岗位。

应聘方式

请将申请材料包括个人简历、主要科研成果、研究兴趣等以 PDF 格式发送至 xumengzhang@fudan.edu.cn，邮件标题请注明：

博后申请+学校+本人姓名
